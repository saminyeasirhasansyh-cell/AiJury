Privacy & data

This public jury room does not store your API key, questions, attachments, or generated reports in a database. Your key is held in this tab's memory and passed through our HTTPS server to OpenAI only when you convene the jury.

Submitted questions, context, files, and generated answers are processed by OpenAI. Requests use store=false; OpenAI's applicable data policies still apply. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request metadata, such as time and IP address. We do not put credentials or case contents in URLs or application logs.

Seat positions are saved in your browser as a preference. Questions and keys are not saved in browser storage. Exported bundles and reports remain wherever you download them. Close or refresh the tab to clear its in-memory key and case. Do not include sensitive information unless you intend to send it to OpenAI.

AI Jury provides simulated perspectives and can make mistakes. Vote shares are not probabilities. You remain responsible for actions taken from a verdict.
